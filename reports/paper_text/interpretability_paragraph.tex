\paragraph{Interpretability.}
Unlike the neural baselines, the LGCP has a small set of parameters with a direct statistical meaning, which in Red-LGCP describe the intensity of active cells (Table~\ref{tab:lgcp_params}, App.~\ref{app:interpretability}); all coefficients have the same sign in every run. Regulatory and calendar factors have the largest effects: the fishing ban reduces the intensity to 10--15\%, public holidays to about 20\% and weekends to 74--82\%. Recent activity raises it by 10\% per count observed on the previous day, and chlorophyll-a, salinity and sea temperature all raise it, chlorophyll-a by 12--13\% per standard deviation. The latent field is mostly a time-constant map of persistent hot spots (80--85\% of its variance), nearly identical in May and November, plus a weekly pattern that refines the weekend indicator.
