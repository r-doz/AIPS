\paragraph{Ablation Study.} To understand which components drive these gains, we remove or replace them one at a time (Table~\ref{tab:results-ablation}, App.~\ref{app:ablation}). The classifier accounts for the advantage in activity detection: without it, or without the gate, accuracy and F1 drop to the level of WGLM, GNN and ConvLSTM, and MAE and Wasserstein distance worsen in all eight weeks. Training the classifier together with the LGCP through the hurdle likelihood, rather than in two stages, improves log-likelihood, CRPS and Wasserstein distance in all eight weeks and makes results more stable across runs. Finally, the uncertainty-aware threshold improves accuracy in seven of the eight weeks, redistribution lowers the Wasserstein distance in all eight without changing the activity decisions, and the kernel improves every metric in May while mattering little in November.
