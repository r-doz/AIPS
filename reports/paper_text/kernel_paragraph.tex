We use a separable covariance obtained by multiplying a spatial and a temporal kernel:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
    k\!\left((\mathbf{s},t),(\mathbf{s}',t')\right)
    ={}& \left(k_{s,\mathrm{broad}}+k_{s,\mathrm{loc}}\right)\\
    &\times\left(k_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}+k_{t,\mathrm{per}}\right),
\end{split}
\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}
where the broad and local spatial components $k_{s,\mathrm{broad}}$ and $k_{s,\mathrm{loc}}$ and the short-range temporal component $k_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}$ are radial basis function (RBF) kernels and $k_{t,\mathrm{per}}$ is a periodic kernel ($c\in\{\mathrm{broad},\mathrm{loc}\}$):
\begin{equation}
    k_{s,c}(\mathbf{s},\mathbf{s}') = \sigma^2_{s,c}\exp\!\left(-\frac{\|\mathbf{s}-\mathbf{s}'\|^2}{2\ell_{s,c}^2}\right),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    k_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}(t,t') = \sigma^2_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{(t-t')^2}{2\ell_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}^2}\right),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    k_{t,\mathrm{per}}(t,t') = \sigma^2_{t,\mathrm{per}}\exp\!\left[-\frac{2\sin^2\!\left(\pi|t-t'|/p\right)}{\ell_{t,\mathrm{per}}^2}\right].
\end{equation}

The spatial kernels act on the standardized cell coordinates and the temporal kernels on standardized time, so the RBF lengthscales are expressed in standardized units. The broad spatial kernel $k_{s,\mathrm{broad}}$ ($\ell_{s,\mathrm{broad}}=0.2$) correlates neighboring cells, whereas the local kernel $k_{s,\mathrm{loc}}$ ($\ell_{s,\mathrm{loc}}=0.01$, far smaller than the distance between neighboring cells) gives each cell its own deviation, uncorrelated with those of the other cells.
Similarly, the temporal RBF $k_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}$ ($\ell_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}=0.01$, about 1.4--2 days) represents short-term, non-recurrent variation, while the periodic kernel $k_{t,\mathrm{per}}$ captures recurrent patterns; its lengthscale is fixed to $\ell_{t,\mathrm{per}}=0.8$ and its period $p$ is initialized at 14 days and subsequently learned.
The variances $\sigma^2_{s,\mathrm{broad}}$, $\sigma^2_{s,\mathrm{loc}}$, $\sigma^2_{t,\mathrm{RBF}}$ and $\sigma^2_{t,\mathrm{per}}$ are also optimized, whereas all lengthscales are fixed.
